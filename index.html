01 / Featured Work

Origin Autonomy

Origin builds an autonomous mobile manipulator for interior construction — a swerve-drive base, a vertical lift, and a UR10e arm carrying spray and sanding tools. My work sits at the intersection of simulation, low-level control, and edge systems.

Primary project · Sim ↔ Real fidelity

Digital Twin & Simulation Fidelity

Isaac Sim · PhysX · ros2_control

Built a high-fidelity digital twin in Isaac Sim to enable reliable validation of robot behaviour across simulation and real-world deployments.

  • Focused on aligning kinematics, dynamics, sensing, and control to ensure consistency between simulated and physical systems.
  • Modelled complex physical interactions and validated control and safety pipelines against real-world behaviour.
  • Made simulation a trustworthy tool for testing and iteration — the anchor for everything downstream.

Distributed systems · Edge compute

Distributed HIL Pipeline

Isaac Sim · Dual Jetson Orins · ROS 2

Developed a hardware-in-the-loop system connecting Isaac Sim with a distributed dual-Orin compute setup, replicating the real robot architecture.

  • Designed pipelines for realistic sensor emulation, communication, and synchronisation across machines.
  • Handled high-bandwidth data streams and ensured stable, real-time operation under full system load.
  • Enabled end-to-end validation of robotics software in a production-like environment — long before it touches a job site.

Motion planning · Design validation

Tool Coverage Analysis Pipeline

MoveIt 2 · IK · Collision checking · Isaac Sim

Built a pipeline to evaluate robot design feasibility by computing tool coverage across large-scale construction site geometries.

  • Generated dense pose sets and validated feasibility using inverse kinematics, collision checking, and task-specific constraints.
  • Aggregated results into quantitative coverage metrics to identify design limitations and guide iteration.
  • Supports scalable evaluation of robot performance across diverse real-world scenarios.
